# Harmonious Us 0.6.8-alpha — Characters, Clothing, Building & a Painted World

Build 68, channel alpha, **protocol 3** (unchanged). A 0.6.8 server asks for a 0.6.8 game: an older game does not know the new bodies or what anyone is wearing, so the launcher will update you before you join.

Everything about clothes is for the game a server runs - a shared server, and **Play alone**. A game started with NEW GAME on the title screen is the older solo game: it gets the new bodies, drawn in plain clothes of your colours, and nothing else about it changes.

## In short

- New characters: a man and a woman with real faces, a build, left- or right-handedness, ten skin tones, seven hairstyles and two beards.
- **Your character is still your character.** Pack, research, professions, home, settlement: all exactly as you left them.
- Clothes are real things: made, carried, traded, stored, dyed - and worn. Forty of them, in eleven places on the body.
- Building: drag out a whole wall, floor or roof at once, Quick Roof [G], clean continuous roofs, ladders you can climb, take one piece away.
- A painted world: new ground, grass and flowers, trees, rocks, cliffs, water and light; 150 buildable pieces drawn with new models.
- Every item has its own icon. Animals look smooth. The game's own flag, banner and rug designs.
- A studio intro, a photosensitivity warning and a Reduce Flashing Effects setting.

## Your character after the update

- Whatever body you had, you now have one of the two new ones: the earlier woman becomes the woman, anyone else the man. Your name, skin and hair colour are kept.
- You are given a plain shirt, trousers and shoes - worn, not taken from or added to your pack.
- The first time you stand in the world the Wardrobe opens on **Appearance** and says what has and has not changed. Choose a body, skin, hair and beard, or keep what you see. This is shown once; you can come back to the page whenever you like.
- Nothing else about your character is read or written by the change.

## Making a character

- Start from one of six presets, or choose: body, build, left- or right-handed, eye colour, ten skin tones, seven hairstyles, hair colour, beard (none, short, full).
- Faces have painted lips, lash lines and brows; the eyes have real irises.
- A left-handed character works with the left hand: every tool is held, swung and carried on the other side.

## The Wardrobe

- **Clothes:** the eleven places - head, face, neck, shirt, coat, hands, waist, legs, feet, back, cloak - what is on each, what in your pack could go there and what it does. PUT ON, TAKE OFF. Putting something on where something is already worn swaps them in one step.
- **Appearance:** body, skin, hair, hair colour, beard. The picture shows what you have chosen, marked TRYING IT ON, and nothing changes for anyone until you press APPLY. RESET and CANCEL do what they say.
- The picture turns and looks closer.
- Below it: what everything you have on adds up to, and what it feels like where you stand right now.
- Under everything, always, are plain undergarments. They are part of the body - not an item, and not something that comes off.

## What clothes do

- **Chilly** (it feels below freezing) or **hot** (above 38): stamina comes back a quarter slower. That is all - neither hurts. Origin's nights are chilly in plain cloth and comfortable in hides or furs; its noon is hot in a fur coat.
- **Warmth** counts against the cold. In the heat, warm clothes cost two fifths of their warmth - and **cooling** (a sun hat, a headwrap, a light shirt) takes heat off.
- **Rain:** out in it and not dressed for it you are soaked, and feel eight degrees colder. A rain cloak or a weather coat, by itself, keeps you dry.
- A roof and a lit fire help, as they always have.
- On a planet whose cold or heat does hurt, the same clothes count against it.
- **Work clothes** take some of the effort out of a tool stroke (up to 30 % in all).
- **The clothes of a trade** teach that trade 5-8 % faster.
- **A backpack** gives stamina back 10 % faster, a large one 20 %. (Your pack has no size limit in this game, so a backpack does not change how much you carry.)
- A recipe says exactly what its garment does; those words come from the numbers the server uses.
- Dust goggles and the face covering do nothing on Origin, and say so.

## Making clothes

A new **Clothing** group in the Craft tab.

- **By hand, from fibre and hide:** cloth wrap, hide tunic, hide trousers, hide shoes, sun hat, belt.
- **Hunting:** fur hood, fur coat, fur gloves, fur-lined boots.
- **Tailoring** (new, 5 research) at a loom: cloth shirt and trousers, light shirt, desert headwrap, face covering, rain cloak, farmer's hat, cook's apron, scarf, bandana, feathered cap.
- **Leatherworking** at a workbench: leather boots, mining gloves, protective gloves, woodworker's apron, reinforced work boots, utility belt.
- **Outfitting** (new, 9 research, after Tailoring) at a workbench: farmer's workwear, fishing vest, craftsperson's apron, hunting jacket, researcher's coat, explorer's jacket, insulated workwear, heavy insulated jacket, weather coat, cold-weather trousers, dust goggles, large backpack.
- The Backpack you could already make can now be worn.

## Dye

- Fourteen colours. Pick one in the Wardrobe to see it on; DYE IT uses one Dye (made by hand from fibre).
- A dye changes the colour and nothing else.
- The dye stays in a garment while you wear it; taking it off washes it out. WASH IT OUT does that without taking it off.

## Tools in your hands

- Twenty-five tools have new models, held in a closed hand, with their own strokes: chop, swing, dig, scoop, till, reap, saw, hammer, cut, pour, cast, draw a bow.
- Long tools ride on the shoulder when carried.
- **First person** now shows your own arms, sleeves and gloves holding the tool, and the view follows your eyes down onto the work.

## Building

- **Drag to build a run:** hold the button (RT on a controller) on the first edge or corner, drag, let go - a row of walls, an area of foundations or floors, a roof over an area. Each section is shown green or red (with the reason), with the count and the materials it takes. Up to 64 sections at once.
- **Quick Roof [G]** (LT on a controller): the room you are aiming at, or press again for the whole floor of the building.
- **Roofs** join into one continuous roof: no more speckled patches where two roof pieces met.
- **Ladders** stand upright against a wall, the side of a pit or a cliff, and stack on each other; climb by walking toward them, jump to let go.
- **Taking a piece away:** X marks only the piece under the crosshair, X again takes it. A piece with a door, shutters, trim or a crate on it names them first; a second press takes it and them, each back to your pack.
- Every section is still its own piece, saved exactly as before.

## The world

- Ground painted cell to cell, meadow grass and flowers, dug ground with soft edges, a dark world underground.
- New trees, bushes, rocks, cliffs and escarpments, reeds, ferns, clouds, water and snow; shingled roofs, painted wood and stone on buildings.
- 150 buildable pieces - furniture, workstations, storage, lights and fires, village and farm pieces, water, power, signals, decorations, production and settlement pieces, paths - are drawn with new models. What they do, where they stand and what you bump into are unchanged.
- Flags, banners and rugs carry the game's own emblems and patterns.
- Animals are shaded smooth instead of faceted.
- Every item has an icon drawn from its own model, in the hotbar, pack, crates, crafting, trade, settlement and wardrobe.

## Starting the game

- The Rooster Roost Studios intro plays after Unity's splash (Space or a pad button skips it).
- A photosensitivity warning is shown on first start and can be read again from Settings > Graphics.
- **Reduce Flashing Effects** (Settings > Graphics) softens lightning and flashes.

## Other players

- Everyone sees your body, hair, clothes and colours, at once when they change, and when they arrive after you.
- A hat flattens the hair under it and a hood hides it. A closed coat hides the shirt under it - you are still wearing the shirt, and it still counts.

## Things to know

- The woman's body offers a beard in the creator and the Wardrobe, which shows nothing.
- Gloves are mitt-shaped: the fingers inside close, the glove does not show them.
- Coats are hip length: there are no long skirts or robes yet.
- Some pieces keep their earlier look: conveyors and lifts, the crop plot, settlement text signs and boards, structural trims, space pieces, and anything with moving parts.
- Choosing your own banner design is not in this version.
- In a world you play alone, characters left behind by sessions before 0.6.7 are still in the world's file, untouched. What to do with them is for 0.6.9.

## For server owners

- No world migration. Every addition to the save is a new field; a 0.6.7 world loads unchanged, and each character is brought to the new bodies the first time its own player joins.
- Two new requests (building a run, taking a piece with what is on it) are additive; protocol stays 3.
- Going back to 0.6.7 afterwards is not supported: restore the backup taken before the update.
