# Harmonious Us 0.6.0-alpha — Six worlds (select alpha testing)

This build is for the selected alpha testers it was handed to. It is not generally available yet.

Build 60, channel alpha, protocol 1. Servers of this build accept clients from 0.6.0-alpha: **a 0.5.1-alpha client must update** (the launcher does it; the game says so if it is tried).

This is an alpha. It is meant to be played, and it is not finished. What is thin or missing is listed at the end, not hidden.

## In short

- Six planets on one server, each its own world: land, plants, rock, animals, sky, water and weather. Five can be reached in this build; the sixth, Astraea, exists but no vessel can be launched for it yet.
- Build a space program, load a colony vessel and cross to another world without leaving the server.
- Heat, cold and airlessness are real: shade, a roof, a fire and what you carry decide how long you last.
- Hunting and a first crop work online. Each planet gives materials found nowhere else.
- You make your character the first time you join a server, and you start with an empty pack.

## The six worlds

| | Land | What grows and stands | Who lives there | Only there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | Meadow, forest, river valleys, a northern range; cliffs show their strata | Oak, fir, pine, birch; the nature it always had | Deer, reindeer, boar, wolf, bear, fox, rabbit, goat, goose and more | — |
| **Khepra** | Banded red mesas, dune fields, black volcanic fields, two smoking volcanoes with moving lava | Saguaro, umbrella tree, acacia, basalt columns, thorn bush, agave, barrel cactus | Dune Strider, Sand Hare, Burrow Rat, Sand Viper, Ember Boar, Ash Jackal, Oasis Fish | Obsidian, sulfur, cactus pulp |
| **Nivara** | Snow to the valley floors, drift ridges, sharp peaks of slate and blue ice; an aurora at night | Frostwood, snow-laden conifers, ice spires, frost bush, ice flowers | Frost Elk, Snow Hare, Frost Fox, Snow Goose, Ice Seal, Snow Stalker, Rime Bear, Ice Fish | Ice shards, frostbloom |
| **Pelagos** | Low green land, a shallow turquoise shelf sea dotted with islands | Palm, mangrove, coral heads, limestone, fern, hibiscus | Tide Crab, Lagoon Seal, Sea Goose, Mangrove Otter, Palm Hog, Sea Krait, Reef Dolphin, Reef Fish | Coral, palm fronds |
| **Viridia** | Overgrown lowland, karst towers, blackwater under a green overcast sky | Giant mushrooms, canopy giants, bulb trees, giant fronds, glowcaps, orchids, mossy rock | Glade Deer, Vine Glider, Root Crawler, Canopy Viper, Marsh Heron, Spore Boar, Shade Stalker, Moss Bear, Blackwater Fish | Resin, glow spores |
| **Astraea** (not reachable yet) | Craters and angular terraces of charcoal and violet stone, still violet brine, no air, stars by day | Nothing grows: crystal clusters, monoliths, crystal spires as tall as towers | Shard Crab, Dust Skitter, and the rare Geode Hulk | Crystal, rare ore |

- Each planet has its own sky (day and night), its own water, its own particles in the air, and its own names for the things of its land.
- Many animals carry something of their planet on their bodies: a ridge of embers, ice along the spine, glowing caps, a crown of crystal. Hostile animals bristle.
- A planet's material is found beside the common one when you gather there, and each has a use: obsidian melts into glass, coral burns into mortar, rare ore smelts into steel, ice melts into water, palm frond twists into rope, frostbloom spins into cloth, resin and sulfur refine into fuel, crystal grinds into electronics, glow spores rot into fertilizer, cactus pulp presses into water.

## Also new since 0.5.1-alpha

**Travel and civilization**

- One dedicated server holds every planet. Travelling is not a reconnect: the same connection, the same character, a new world. A crossing takes time and the screen says where you are going.
- A Planet page shows where a planet's civilization stands: seven pillars, a lifecycle stage, and what the next stage asks.
- The colony vessel is a ship you go aboard: twenty-one metres tall with a hull five metres across, a boarding ramp, a doorway, a cabin deck with four seats and lashed cargo, a ring of passenger windows and a flight deck. Walk up the ramp and load it from inside.
- The space program is six buildable parts. A colony vessel has a hold; each destination asks for a manifest. When it is aboard, the vessel carries you and whoever stands beside it, up to four. Arriving sets down a beacon and a supply cache. A colony can build its own vessel and sail home.
- The Fuel Refinery turns coal (or resin, or sulfur) into industrial fuel. Spacecraft propellant is a category of its own, made from refined fuel at a Propellant Plant.
- A launch is seen. When a colony vessel leaves, everyone on that planet watches it: the engines light, the ship lifts and climbs out of sight. If you are aboard you watch from beside the pad, and then the crossing screen takes over. The vessel itself stays on its pad for the next crossing.

**Surviving a planet**

- No air on Astraea (a minute of breath), heat on Khepra by day, cold on Nivara. Shade helps against heat; a roof and a lit fire against cold. The HUD shows AIR, HEAT or COLD when it matters.

**Hunting and farming (alpha)**

- Wildlife lives in online worlds. Craft a hunting spear, a bow and arrows by hand. A killed animal leaves a carcass; harvesting it gives meat, hide, bone, antler or feather, granted by the server. Predators bite, and it counts; leather armour, carried, takes two fifths off.
- A Crop Plot grows seed into food, three measures for each seed. Growth is kept by the server and survives a restart. Where the air is too hot or too cold a plot needs what you need. Nothing grows on Astraea.

**How it looks**

- Buildings were redrawn. Walls stand on a sill beam or a stone plinth and close with a plate or coping; stone and brick have dressed corner blocks; doorways and windows have heavy frames, lintels and sills, and windows have panes. Roofs are their own material (dark shake over timber, slate over stone, terracotta over brick) with edge boards and a heavy ridge. Floors, foundations, pillars, stairs and ramps have rims, kerbs, footings and newels.
- Khepra's lava is a liquid: lakes that heave, crust over toward their shores and bubble, and streams whose crust is carried downhill. It lights its crater at night. **It burns**: standing in a lava lake costs a fifth of your health every half second.
- Gathering throws off what the thing is made of: wood chips and leaves, stone fragments, sparks from ore, coal dust.
- Other players are seen working: the axe or pickaxe in their hand, and the swing, as they chop and mine.
- Hearth and campfire smoke is soft puffs now, not squares against the night sky.
- The soundtrack gained a launch cue (Short Looped Logo #2 from the game's music pack).
- A felled tree leaves log sections lying where it fell. Pick each one up (E) for two more wood, on top of what the chopping gave.
- Graphics settings: a quality preset, shadows, vegetation distance, effects, water, anti-aliasing, textures and render scale. Display settings: fullscreen, borderless or windowed, resolution, v-sync, frame limit.

**Joining and playing**

- The first time you join a server you make your character there; the server validates and keeps it. You start with an empty pack. Reconnecting gives you the same character back.
- A public world and a test world can never be mixed up: test tools refuse to run against a world that is not a test world.

**The launcher**

- The launcher is how the game is installed and kept current: INSTALL, PLAY, UPDATE, REPAIR. Downloads are checked by size and SHA-256 before anything is installed; the previous version is kept and put back if an update fails; your worlds and settings are never touched.
- Launcher 0.3.0 shows what is new, the download size and speed, and asks a second public address if the first does not answer.

## Fixed

- A connection that closed and another that opened in the same moment could be confused by the server.
- The keyboard stayed captured if a session ended with a device panel open.
- Saving a server with six planets took most of a tick; planets on which nothing happened are no longer rewritten.

## Known limitations (alpha)

- **Animals are simulated by each player's own game.** Two players do not see the same deer in the same place. The server owns what a hunt yields, arrows and bite damage, and limits how fast harvests can be claimed; it does not yet own the animals. Breeding and domestication are not in this build.
- **Animal bodies are shared between planets.** Each planet's animals have their own names, colours, sizes, roles and what they carry on their backs, but under that they are the same dozen-and-a-half bodies. **The reindeer is a placeholder**: it is the deer's body and movement, larger and greyer. The two crabs carry no adornment. New creature models are future work.
- **Log sections are drawn by each player's own game.** The server decides how many logs a felled tree left (three, or two for a dead tree) and gives the wood; where each log lies differs between players. Logs not picked up within ten minutes, or before a server restart, are gone.
- **Farming is one crop in one kind of plot.** No watering, no seasons, no planet-specific crops, no sealed or hydroponic farming.
- **Each planet material has one use**, in a machine that already existed. There are no planet-specific recipes or building materials yet: every planet builds in wood, stone and brick.
- **Buildings** are one architectural style in three materials (timber, stone, brick) on every planet. Window panes are drawn, not openable. Furniture, workstations and machines were not redrawn in this pass.
- **Player characters are temporary.** The current models and clothes are stand-ins and were not reworked; a customizable character system is planned to replace them.
- **Performance was measured on one high-end PC only** (Radeon RX 7900 XTX, Core i9-12900K, 1920x1080): above 200 frames a second on every planet at Low, Medium and High, with no frame over 50 ms while standing and looking around. It has not been measured on mid-range, laptop or integrated graphics, and the first seconds after arriving on a planet were not measured. If it runs badly for you, the GRAPHICS settings (Low preset, render scale) are the first thing to try.
- Terrain is drawn in four-metre faces: cliffs are banded slopes, not sculpted rock. Lava does not spread or cool, there is no heat shimmer, and only the crater LAKES burn - a lava stream on a volcano's flank can be walked through unharmed, which it should not be.
- **Astraea cannot be reached by players.** Its pressure-habitat program is not buildable, so no vessel can be launched for it. It exists on the server and was only ever visited with test tools. Pelagos and Viridia have ordinary air and no hazard of their own.
- **Only the vessel's cabin deck can be entered.** The decks above (passenger ring, flight deck) are seen from outside. A vessel placed on a slope has a ramp that does not meet the ground evenly. The cabin walls are solid to your own game but not yet to the server.
- Fuel and propellant machines need neither power nor water. Nothing burns fuel in an engine yet. Feathers have no use yet.
- Day and night are local to each game, not a shared clock.
- Bringing a planet into memory for the first time after a server start stalls the server for about a seventh of a second.
- The launcher and the game are not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen will warn.
- **Linux: ALPHA / LIMITED REAL-DESKTOP TESTING.** The Linux client is built and packaged by the same process as the Windows one, but it has not been run on a real Linux desktop. The Linux launcher is a terminal program. Expect problems, and please report them.
- From 0.5.1-alpha: no vertical adjustment of a placement, no thirst, no player trading, no sit or play animations, remote players' held items are not drawn.
